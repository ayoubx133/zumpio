About the game

Big smiling balls bounce around the screen. Slide your little cannon left and right while it pops them with pellets. Big balls split into smaller ones, so keep moving and clear the whole wave.

  • Drag to move, shooting is automatic
  • Balls split into smaller balls
  • Waves that grow with each level
  • Lots of levels that get harder step by step
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Bright colors, happy sounds and juicy effects
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
