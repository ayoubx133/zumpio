About the game

Three shapes, one board. Drag each wooden block into place. Fill a whole row or column and it bursts away to make more room. Clear enough lines to pass the level, but keep space free: if no shape fits, the round is over.

  • Relaxing drag and drop
  • No timer, think as long as you like
  • Satisfying line clears
  • Lots of levels that get harder step by step
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Bright colors, happy sounds and juicy effects
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
