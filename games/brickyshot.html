About the game

Drag to aim and let go. Your balls bounce around the board and chip away at the numbered bricks. Collect extra balls, find the clever angles and clear the board before the bricks reach the bottom.

  • Aim with a clear guide line
  • Collect more balls as you go
  • Relaxing angle puzzle action
  • Lots of levels that get harder step by step
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Bright colors, happy sounds and juicy effects
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
