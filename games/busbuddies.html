About the game

The buses are waiting and the passengers are all mixed up. Tap a passenger with a free path to send it to the bus of its color. If its bus is not there yet, it waits on the bench, but the bench only has five seats. Fill every bus to win.

  • Tap to move passengers
  • Plan the order to keep the bench free
  • Every level has a solution
  • Lots of levels that get harder step by step
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Bright colors, happy sounds and juicy effects
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
