About the game

Slide left and right to collect a long line of cups. Run them through gates that add lids, sleeves and juice to make them worth more. Dodge the obstacles and serve your drinks for coins.

  • Easy swipe steering
  • Gates that upgrade your cups
  • Happy customers at the finish
  • Lots of levels that get harder step by step
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Bright colors, happy sounds and juicy effects
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
