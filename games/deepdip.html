About the game

Steer the hook past the fish on the way down to go deeper. On the way back up, catch everything you can. At the surface your fish turn into coins. Upgrade your line and dive again.

  • Drag to steer the hook
  • Dive deeper with upgrades
  • Lots of cute fish to catch
  • Lots of levels that get harder step by step
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Bright colors, happy sounds and juicy effects
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
