About the game

Draw with your finger to dig a tunnel through the sand. The balls roll down your tunnel to the little truck waiting below. Touch the white balls on the way to color them in. Deliver enough balls to finish the level.

  • Dig with your finger
  • Balls roll and bounce for real
  • Find the best path
  • Lots of levels that get harder step by step
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Bright colors, happy sounds and juicy effects
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
