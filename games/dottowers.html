About the game

Drag from your blue tower to another tower to send half of your dots there. Capture gray and red towers when you have more dots than they do. The red side does the same, so think fast and grow.

  • Drag to send your dots
  • Towers grow over time
  • Simple strategy for all ages
  • Lots of levels that get harder step by step
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Bright colors, happy sounds and juicy effects
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
