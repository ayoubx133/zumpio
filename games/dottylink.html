Connect the matching dots with pipes and fill the whole board!

About the game

Every color has two dots. Draw a pipe to join each pair, but pipes can not cross, and the whole board has to be filled. Boards start small and grow as you get better.

  • Draw pipes with your finger
  • Boards from 5x5 up to 9x9
  • Every board has a solution
  • Lots of levels that get harder step by step
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Bright colors, happy sounds and juicy effects
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
