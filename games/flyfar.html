About the game

Pull back, let go and send your paper plane into the sky. Hold to lift the nose, fly through the rings and ride the wind. Spend your coins on upgrades and fly even farther next time.

  • Drag to launch, hold to glide
  • Rings and wind gusts
  • Upgrades between flights
  • Lots of levels that get harder step by step
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Bright colors, happy sounds and juicy effects
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
