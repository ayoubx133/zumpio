Flick the ball toward the goal and bend it around the goalkeeper. Aim for the corners to hit the bonus targets. Score enough of your five shots to win.

  • Lots of levels that get harder step by step

  • Bright colors, happy sounds and juicy effects
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
