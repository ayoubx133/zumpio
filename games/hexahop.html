About the game

Drag stacks of colorful hexagon tiles onto the board. When two stacks with the same color on top sit side by side, the tiles hop across one by one and join up. Build a stack of ten and it pops. Pop enough tiles before the board fills up.

  • Satisfying hop and merge animation
  • Simple to start, tricky to master
  • Calm puzzle with no timer
  • Lots of levels that get harder step by step
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Bright colors, happy sounds and juicy effects
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
