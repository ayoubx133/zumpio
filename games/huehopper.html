About the game

Your ball can only slip through the part of each ring that matches its color. Tap to hop, wait for the right moment and jump through. Stars change your color along the way.

  • One tap controls
  • Spinning rings, bars and crosses
  • Quick restart keeps it fun
  • Lots of levels that get harder step by step
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Bright colors, happy sounds and juicy effects
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
