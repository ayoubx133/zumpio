About the game

Tap a column to drop a wobbly jelly block. Two blocks with the same number melt into one bigger number. Plan your drops, set up chain reactions and build the target number before a column overflows.

  • Tap to drop, watch them merge
  • Chain reactions for big combos
  • A friendly way to play with numbers
  • Lots of levels that get harder step by step
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Bright colors, happy sounds and juicy effects
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
