About the game

Tap a tube, tap another, and watch the colored water pour. You can only pour onto the same color or into an empty tube, so every move counts. Sort all the colors and the level is done. Stuck? Undo a move or use the spare tube.

  • Satisfying pouring animation
  • Undo button and a spare tube
  • Every level can be solved
  • Lots of levels that get harder step by step
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Bright colors, happy sounds and juicy effects
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
