About the game

Sun and sunglasses. Bee and flower. Key and lock. Find the two pictures that go together and connect them with your finger. A right match pops with stars, a wrong one costs a heart. A fun first thinking game for little ones.

  • Draw lines with one finger
  • Cute pictures that belong together
  • Great for early learners
  • Lots of levels that get harder step by step
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Bright colors, happy sounds and juicy effects
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
