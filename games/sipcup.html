About the game

The tap is about to open and the cup is thirsty. Draw a line so the water drops slide and bounce into the cup. Fill it up to the dotted line to win. Use less ink for three stars.

  • Draw with your finger
  • Real flowing water drops
  • Stars for clever short lines
  • Lots of levels that get harder step by step
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Bright colors, happy sounds and juicy effects
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
