About the game

The smiling red block wants out. Slide the other wooden blocks left, right, up or down to clear its path to the exit. Fewer moves earn more stars.

  • Classic sliding block fun
  • Move counter and three stars
  • Every puzzle is checked to be solvable
  • Lots of levels that get harder step by step
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Bright colors, happy sounds and juicy effects
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
