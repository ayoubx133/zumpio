About the game

Hold your finger to smash through the colorful platforms. Let go to bounce safely. Black slices stop you, unless you smash long enough to power up the fire ball that breaks everything.

  • Hold and release controls
  • Fire mode smashes through anything
  • Towers get taller and faster
  • Lots of levels that get harder step by step
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Bright colors, happy sounds and juicy effects
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
