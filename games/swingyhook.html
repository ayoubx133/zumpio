About the game

Hold your finger to grab the nearest hook with your rope. Swing, release at the right moment and fly to the next one. Bounce pads give you an extra lift.

  • Hold and release controls
  • Real pendulum swinging
  • Short levels, quick restarts
  • Lots of levels that get harder step by step
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Bright colors, happy sounds and juicy effects
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
