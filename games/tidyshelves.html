About the game

The shop shelves are a mess. Drag juice, jam, soap and more between the shelves. Three of the same item side by side vanish with a sparkle, and the hidden items behind move forward. Tidy everything before time runs out.

  • Drag and drop sorting
  • Hidden back rows to uncover
  • Cute shop goods to match
  • Lots of levels that get harder step by step
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Bright colors, happy sounds and juicy effects
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
