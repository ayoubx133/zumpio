About the game

A lamp shines a beam across the board. Tap the mirrors to turn them and bounce the light around corners. Guide the beam through every star to win the level.

  • Tap to rotate mirrors
  • The beam updates instantly
  • A gentle first physics puzzle
  • Lots of levels that get harder step by step
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Bright colors, happy sounds and juicy effects
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
