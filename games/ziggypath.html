About the game

The ball rolls on a narrow path in the sky. Tap at the right moment to change direction at each corner. Collect gems and reach the finish without rolling off the edge.

  • Gems to collect
  • Speed picks up as you improve
  • Lots of levels that get harder step by step
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Bright colors, happy sounds and juicy effects
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
